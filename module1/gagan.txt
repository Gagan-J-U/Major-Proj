# And there is an especially useful baseline

The published work provides us with a very useful reference point.

They trained a Whisper encoder on Switchboard and evaluated zero-shot on FluencyBank Timestamped. Their audio-based model's weighted recall was **0.65 on Switchboard but 0.43 on FluencyBank Timestamped**, illustrating the domain/generalization problem. Repetition was particularly difficult. ([PubMed Central (PMC)][1])

That gives us a very interesting research question:

```text
Typical speech training
        ↓
Poorer generalization
        ↓
Speech from PWS
```

Can we improve that by training/fine-tuning on appropriate PWS data?

That's far more interesting than simply building a detector.
